Ask for help and follow it on your phone.

For households, barangay halls and organizations. The services shown depend on your account type.

  • Book an ambulance in five short steps.
  • Request a service such as road clearing, relief goods, training or certification.
  • Borrow equipment for pickup or delivery, or ask for an item that is not listed.
  • Track every request with a clear status and the office's reason.
  • Read safety guides offline, find hotlines and get alerts, in English or Filipino.

Download SERBIS for Android

Built for MDRRMO Echague, Municipality of Echague, Isabela.

SERBIS is not on the Play Store, so you install it from a file. Your phone or Google Play Protect may warn you about an unknown app. That is expected for apps outside the Play Store. The steps on the right show what to tap.

Download the app Version 1.0.0 · 56.5 MB · Android 7.0 or newer · Use Wi-Fi if you can

On a computer? Open serbis-landing-page.vercel.app on your Android phone to install.

Android phones only. iPhones are not supported. Need help installing? Call 0917 626 2352 or visit the MDRRMO office.

How to install

  1. Tap Download the app and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or Downloads.
  3. If your phone says it can't install unknown apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Open SERBIS and create your account with your mobile number.
  6. Organizations: wait for MDRRMO to activate your account. To follow up, call 0917 626 2352.

The steps can look different on each phone brand.

Sa Filipino

  1. I-tap ang Download the app at hintaying matapos ang file.
  2. Buksan ang file mula sa notifications o sa Downloads.
  3. Kung sabihin ng telepono na hindi ito makapag-install mula sa source na ito, i-tap ang Settings, i-on ang Allow from this source, bumalik, at i-tap ang Install.
  4. Kung magbabala ang Google Play Protect, i-tap ang More details, saka ang Install anyway.
  5. Buksan ang SERBIS at gumawa ng account gamit ang inyong mobile number.
  6. Mga organisasyon: hintaying i-activate ng MDRRMO ang inyong account. Para mag-follow up, tumawag sa 0917 626 2352.

Run the office queue from one screen.

For MDRRMO staff. A web panel for the office queue. A super admin chooses which sections each staff account can open.

  • Resident requests. Review, approve, disapprove, resolve, or file a walk-in request.
  • Ambulance dispatch and equipment borrowing. Assign units, reschedule, print the conduction form, release items with a due date and confirm returns.
  • Fleet, responders and stock. Keep units, staff records and equipment up to date.
  • Accounts, documents, text blast and hotlines, with a dashboard, analytics and activity logs.
